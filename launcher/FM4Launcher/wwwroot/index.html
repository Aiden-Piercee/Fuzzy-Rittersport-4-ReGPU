About FM4:ReGPU

A native PC port of Forza Motorsport 4 (Xbox 360). The game's PowerPC code is statically recompiled to x64, it runs on our own implementation of the Xbox 360 kernel, and a native Direct3D 12 renderer (ReGPU) replaces the console's GPU.

No game data is included: it plays from your own copy of the disc.

Built on FM2:ReGPU, the Forza Motorsport 2 port, and shares its kernel, renderer and launcher.

Devices

Every wheel, pedal set, shifter and handbrake the game sees. Pedals, a shifter and a handbrake on their own USB device are read together with the wheel. Click one to watch its inputs.

Bindings

Press Bind, then turn, press or pull the control on any device. A pedal pressed on a pedal set makes that the pedal device, a gear put in on a shifter makes that the shifter device, a handbrake pulled on its own device makes that the handbrake device. Esc cancels.

Shader cache

Every shader in the game (about 1,500, from its effect files and the executable) is compiled on the first start, in about three and a half minutes, and kept here so races don't stutter later.
